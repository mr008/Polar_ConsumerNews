## 3. Decide

Nothing here clears the bar for a config change. These are proposals only, and I'm applying nothing.

1. **Propose a same-story dedup guard (code PR).**
   - Evidence: three duplicates in four weeks (Ernesto/polarbear 10-22, romanbuildsaas/polarbear 10-28/29, marclou/polarbear 11-14). The polarbear copy got 5v vs 15v for the original.
   - Change: before drafting, compare the candidate to posts published in the last 48 hours, by shared key numbers or entities (for example "$9K" and "$1.5K"). Skip the candidate on a match. This also covers the case where a qt source and a web source cover the same story.
   - Predicted effect: no same-story pairs within 48 hours. Per-post reach is unchanged, since the duplicate adds almost nothing.
   - Revert condition: if the guard blocks more than 1 in 10 drafts, or blocks two distinct stories that share a number, loosen the match.

2. **Close both experiments and pick a default (config proposal for the owner).**
   - Evidence: both are decided as no difference at day 38 and 39, with n above 10 per arm.
   - Change: set `image_card` and `author_bait` to `enabled: false`, so the default is text with the h/t tail. That is cheaper and has fewer failure points (the media upload path is still unproven in production).
   - Predicted effect: median 24h views stay at about 6. Render and upload failures drop to zero.
   - Revert condition: median 24h views fall below 4 over 14 days after the change.

3. **Add a stale-pipeline alert (code PR).**
   - Evidence: 6 days with 0 reads and 0 posts, and the 11-09 memo was the first to notice.
   - Change: a daily check that fails loudly (red workflow or notification) when there are 0 posts for 24 hours during a period when the queue holds publishable drafts, or 0 reads for 12 hours.
   - Predicted effect: the next outage is caught within a day, not a week.
   - Revert condition: if it fires on more than 2 days a month without a real outage, raise the threshold.

I'm not proposing a change to follower strategy. The only thing that has moved followers in 6 weeks is the 10-21 to 10-28 step, and I can't isolate its cause.
