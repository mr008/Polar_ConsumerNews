## 3. Decide

At most 3 changes. Each has evidence, a change, a predicted effect and a revert condition.

**1. Close the image_card test: set `experiments.image_card.enabled: false` (text only).**
- Evidence: 24 days, 17 vs 19 posts, median 24h views of 7.0 for text and 4.0 for card. The lift is 43%, over the 30% threshold. Cards also add a failure path that has never been confirmed.
- Predicted effect: median 24h views on text-only posts stays at 6-8 or better. No loss in reach, with less render and upload risk.
- Revert condition: if the 14-day median 24h views after the change falls below 4.0, re-enable the test with a fresh `started:` date.
- Note that disabling the test also stops cards on drafts already queued (CLAUDE.md). That is the intended outcome. This is a config change for a human to apply. I do not apply it.

**2. Close the author_bait test as "no difference" and keep the current mix.**
- Evidence: 25 days, tail 8.0 vs question 6.0 median 24h views. The gap is 25%, under 30%.
- Change: no config change needed beyond setting the test to its default arm weights. I propose leaving the 8 vs 29 split as it is. If a human wants to stop spending effort on it, set the question share to 0. I'm not recommending that, because the question tag is the only mechanism in the pipeline that creates a notification to a source author.
- Predicted effect: none. Revert condition: not applicable.

**3. Propose a code PR: de-duplicate same-story posts across sources.**
- Evidence: the 10-22 pair (Ernesto and polarbeargrowth, same $150k ban story, 2 hours apart) and the 10-28/10-29 pair (romanbuildsaas and polarbeargrowth, same views-vs-subscribers story, about 21 hours apart). Two incidents in two weeks with 2.4 posts per day. Repeating a story to a 17-follower audience wastes a slot and looks like a bot.
- Change: before drafting, compare the candidate against posts from the last 72 hours with a cheap similarity check (shared numbers plus a token overlap). Skip the candidate if it matches. One idea, one PR, with a golden test, and run pytest before proposing. The change touches the select stage and no protected path.
- Predicted effect: 0 same-story pairs within 72 hours. One slot per week returns to the pool, with an expected small gain in median views.
- Revert condition: if the check blocks more than 2 posts per week that a human judges to be different stories, loosen the threshold.

**Not changing:** the teaching weight. The judge does not predict reach, but N is about 30 posts and the topic gate and plain-voice gate are doing their job. I want one more week before touching ranking.
