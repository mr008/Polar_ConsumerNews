## 3. Decide

Two changes clear the bar. One does not.

**Change 1 (propose): end both experiments, with no arm preferred on reach.**
- *Evidence:* both are decided "no difference" on the fixed rule, at day 46 and 47 and well over 10 posts per arm.
- *Change:* set `image_card.enabled: false` and `author_bait.enabled: false`. Keep the default as **text with a tail ending**. The text arm is cheaper because there is no render or upload, and the tail arm is at least as good on reach.
- *Predicted effect:* median 24h views stay at about 6. Cost and failure surface drop slightly.
- *Revert condition:* if median 24h views over the next 21 days fall by more than a third from 6, re-enable `image_card` at 50% and re-grade.
- *Note:* disabling `image_card` also stops cards for drafts already queued, per CLAUDE.md. That is intended.

**Change 2 (propose, code, separate small PR): a duplicate-story guard.**
- *Evidence:* four occurrences in four weeks (10-22, 10-28/29, 11-14, 11-20). On 11-14 the duplicate got 5 views against 15 for the original. On 11-20 the web and qt copies got 7 and 5 for near-identical text.
- *Change:* before publishing, compare the draft against posts from the last 7 days. Use a cheap token-overlap check on the body, or shared numbers and key nouns. If the overlap is above a threshold, mark it `blocked: duplicate_story`. Keep this deterministic with no LLM call, and add a golden test using the 11-14 Somali pair and the 11-20 App Store pair.
- *Predicted effect:* about one fewer post per week. Median reach should not fall, because the duplicates were below the median.
- *Revert condition:* if more than 2 blocks in a week turn out to be false positives on reading the text, raise the threshold. Never weaken the pipeline route.
- *Verify:* run pytest before the PR.

**Not changing: the judge.** The scores don't track reach, but N is about 20 per week and the judge exists for teaching quality, not reach. A change here would be a guess.

**Open question, not a change: distribution.** The median has been about 6 for five weeks. The posts are fine and the base is 17 to 19 followers. I think reach is capped by who sees the posts, and an account of this size gets almost nothing from the feed. The one lever the pack shows is which authors are quoted. I propose no change until the `author_followers` join exists.
